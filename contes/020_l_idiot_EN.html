The man who greased his mustache

A man who did not want to be looked down on because of his poverty had gotten into the habit of greasing his mustache with a nice, fatty sheep’s tail.
Then he would mingle with the rich and important, one hand on his stomach, saying:
“I can’t eat another bite. I’ve been feasting on one rich dish after another.”
And he would run his fingers over his mustache, as if to say:
“Look at my mustache! Still glistening with grease from my last feast.”
His stomach would answer him silently:
“Your boasting is killing me. Curse that greasy mustache of yours!”
His prayer was partly answered...
A cat came along and ran off with the sheep’s tail.
The man’s son chased after the cat, but in vain.
Afraid of being scolded, he ran to the gathering where his father was and cried out:
“Dad! The sheep’s tail you use every morning to grease your mustache... the cat just ran off with it, and I couldn’t catch him!”
At these words, everyone burst out laughing...
But then they invited the man to eat and drink with them...
