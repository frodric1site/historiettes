Gonnella and the pill

A man had only one thing on his mind: to become intelligent.
One day, Gonnella told him:
— With just one pill, I can make you intelligent… and it won’t cost you much.
The man couldn’t believe it. His dream was finally about to come true…
He agreed immediately.
Gonnella then put into his mouth a pill made from a small goat dropping coated with a thin layer of honey.
The man chewed for a moment, suddenly stopped, then spat it onto the ground.
— But this tastes like shit!
Gonnella replied:
— It is! See how intelligent you are now…

And he charged him the agreed price.

About this tale
This story appears in the fifteenth century in Poggio Bracciolini’s Facetiae.
It features Gonnella, a famous Italian jester around whom numerous stories circulated.
The same comic device has survived through the centuries in various forms, including the “intelligence pills” attributed, among others, to Nasreddin Hodja.
