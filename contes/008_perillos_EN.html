Perillos

Phalaris ruled over the Greek city of Acragas.
A craftsman named Perillos came to present him with an invention: a life-sized bronze bull, hollow inside and fitted with a trapdoor.
The condemned would be locked inside, and a fire would be lit beneath its belly.
Thanks to an ingenious system of pipes, the screams of those being burned alive would be transformed into the bellowing of a bull.
Phalaris stared at him impassively.
— It will be a thrilling spectacle for the crowds, said Perillos. Such a punishment will inspire incomparable terror and secure your authority once and for all.
Phalaris stopped him with a gesture of his hand.
— You invented it. It is only right that you should be the first to try it.
Phalaris had Perillos locked inside the bull, and the bellowing began before the fire had even been lit…”
