The monkey and the fish

A monkey was thirsty.
He walked over to a small stream.
There he saw a fish.
— Poor thing! It’s drowning!
He bent down and managed to catch it.
He laid it on the bank.
The fish began to leap about wildly.
— Look how happy he is! thought the monkey.
Then the fish stopped moving.
The monkey gently touched him,
then looked at him sadly.
— If only I had arrived sooner, I could have saved him.
