The gold ring

In the Himalayan region lived a tortoise.
Two young wild geese, who came there in search of food, made her acquaintance, and little by little, the three became great friends.
One day, the two geese said to her:
— Friend Tortoise, we have a magnificent home in a golden cave near the summit of the Himalayas. Would you like to come with us?
— How could I? she asked. It’s much too far for me.
— We’ll take you there, if you promise to keep your mouth shut for the entire journey.
— I promise! Take me with you!
So they had the tortoise bite down on a stick, then each goose took one end of it in her beak, and they flew off.
As they were flying over a village, some children spotted them and cried out:
— Look! Two geese carrying a tortoise on a stick!
When the tortoise heard this, she couldn’t help opening her mouth to shout:
— And if my friends are carrying me, what business is it of yours, you little brats?
She fell into the village square and broke in two.

Indian Buddhist tale — Kacchapa Jātaka (No. 215)
