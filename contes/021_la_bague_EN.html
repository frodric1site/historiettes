The gold ring

Afanti had a friend who was about to leave on a long journey.
As they said their goodbyes, his friend's eyes, though filled with tears, could not stray from the gold ring on Afanti's finger.
“Dear Afanti,” he said, “we have been friends for so many years. If I have to go too long without seeing you, it will be very hard to bear.
Besides, who knows when I will be able to return?
“You could give me your gold ring. That way, whenever I miss you, all I’ll have to do is look at it. It will be as if you were there.”
Afanti, whose only possession of any value was that ring, replied:
“What you say touches my heart, my friend!
“I too will miss you if I have to go too long without seeing you.
“So I think it would be better if I kept the ring.
“Because now, whenever I look at it, I will remember that you asked me for it and that I did not give it to you.
“It will be as if you were there, and I won’t miss you anymore…”
