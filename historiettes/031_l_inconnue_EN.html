L'inconnue

Paris. I'm six or seven years old. I'm coming back from the neighborhood bakery with our baguette for dinner.
On the way, I notice a young woman across the street looking at me.
Intrigued, I look at her again.
She's still looking at me.
Every time I turn my head toward her, she's looking at me.
I decide not to take my eyes off her.
She smiles at me. Her smile is beautiful…
Suddenly, I feel my head come to a dead stop and my body violently thrown backward.
I get up, a little dazed. My baguette is broken in two. One end hangs limply.
The woman is still looking at me.
This time, she laughs. Her laugh is beautiful too…
There was construction work on the sidewalk.
I hadn't seen the construction tape stretched out in front of me.
