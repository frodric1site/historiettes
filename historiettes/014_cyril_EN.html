Cyril

His friend’s name was Cyril.
At the beginning of his second year of primary school, he looked for him but couldn’t find him anywhere.
He asked another child if he had seen him.
The child replied:
— He’s dead. He got hit in the head by a tennis ball.
That made him smile.
Cyril still hadn’t shown up…
Despite his shyness, he dared to speak to the teacher:
— Miss, I can’t find Cyril. Do you know where he is?
She replied, as if she were telling him the time:
— He’s dead.
He waited for him all day…
That evening, he told his mother.
She replied distractedly that she had heard a child had died.
She didn’t know he was his only friend.
Did he ever really exist…
