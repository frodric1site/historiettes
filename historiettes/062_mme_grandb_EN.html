MRS GranB

The teacher is a very old, very thin, very gaunt little woman.
She wears thick glasses.
The classroom is filled with children sitting behind old wooden desks.
Beautiful light streams in through the windows.
I can’t take my eyes off it…
Suddenly... a jolt of electricity shoots through my body from head to toe.
I feel myself lifted from my chair, pulled by the hair by some unknown force, then dragged all the way to the large blackboard.
Once there, I can’t hear anything.
I can’t feel anything.
My vision is blurred by tears.
My trousers are soaking wet.
I don’t understand what just happened…
what I did.
