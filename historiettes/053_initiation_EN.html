Initiation à la musique

Paris, elementary school. My arm is in a cast.
It’s the end of the school day, and our teacher wants to introduce us to one of his favorite pieces of music.
This is serious business. The atmosphere is solemn, almost religious.
The music begins…
The students listen…
or pretend to.
Someone looks at me and gives me a friendly smile.
I smile back.
And then…
My head slams violently into the cast on my arm, resting on the desk.
I’ve just received a monumental slap to the back of the head.
I didn’t know it, but the teacher was standing right behind me.
He wasn’t listening to the music.
He was waiting…
watching for his chance…
Ever since that day, whenever someone wants to introduce me to music,
I think back to the day I kissed my cast.
