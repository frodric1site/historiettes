With a face like that

Paris, near the end of high school.
My girlfriend works in an art supply shop on Place du Tertre in Montmartre.
I’m always hanging around up there.
I help her out a little at work, but mostly I play chess with the portrait artists.
One of them regularly comes into the shop to buy supplies.
Whenever he sees me standing next to my girlfriend, he looks at me and says:
— With a face like that…
He simply can’t understand how, with a face like mine, I could possibly be her boyfriend.
And every time, everyone bursts out laughing.
Except him.
He looks completely serious, almost troubled by it.
Sometimes, when we’re alone, he comes up to me as though he’s about to share a confidence:
— Tell me. How do you do it? With a face like that…

More than forty years later, whenever I hear that expression, I think of him.
And I feel like laughing.
