Monsieur M.

Paris. High school.
I’m walking to school, staring at the pavement, lost in thought.
The avenue is deserted at this late hour of the morning.
Suddenly, a thunderous noise makes me look up.
There, right in front of me: Mr M., the school’s music teacher.
An older man, notorious among the students for his severity.
He quickly looks to the right, then to the left, as if checking that no one heard him.
Then he sees me and freezes.
And when he realizes I’m looking at him too, he gives me a dark look that terrifies me.
I want the ground to swallow me up.
Had he somehow sensed that one day I would write about this…
