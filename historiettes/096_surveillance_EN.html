Night Watch

Paris, June 2026.
It’s the worst of the heat wave.
Lyn and I alternate between showers and half-sleeping on the floor on soaked sheets.
To cool down a little more, we prop our bedroom door open with a shoe, hoping to create some kind of draft from the high window in the hallway.
The hotel and the surrounding area don’t exactly inspire confidence.
Falling asleep with the door open is out of the question.
So we take turns staying awake and keeping an eye on the door.
Unfortunately, the little bit of air coming through feels so good that staying awake in our condition is torture.
It’s Lyn’s turn to keep watch.
He tells me not to worry. He’s scrolling on his phone and isn’t tired at all…
Suddenly, I wake with a start, as if I could sense that something was wrong.
The door is open and Lyn is sleeping like a baby.
He’s snoring his head off…
I burst out laughing, get up, and close the door.
