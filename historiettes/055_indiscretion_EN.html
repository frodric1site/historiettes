Indiscretion

He comes home from class and finds his mother sitting on his bed, reading his private notebook.
He always keeps it carefully hidden behind his bed.
She looks up at him and asks calmly:
— You don’t mind that I read your journal, do you?
He stands there, motionless…
torn between the urge to burst out laughing, a deep sadness… and an overwhelming sense of disillusionment.
He tells her he doesn’t.
Then she adds:
— It helped me to read it.
