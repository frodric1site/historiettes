The Archivist

This morning, my cat accidentally scratched me.
Looking at my hand, a mysterious figure from my childhood suddenly came back to me.

When I was a child, my grandfather would sometimes take us to a restaurant in Chartres.
Whenever we ate there, a man was almost always there too.
From what I overheard, I gathered that he was an archivist and painfully shy.
He was tall, always very well dressed and very reserved.
He always smiled, was very polite, and often laughed rather strangely, almost mechanically, whenever he spoke to someone.
His hands were covered in cat scratches.
I heard the whispers and mockery directed at him, and I laughed too.
Yet I had the feeling that he saw all of it, and that none of it held the slightest interest for him.
I watched him, fascinated.
I wondered how he did it…
how he managed to live so well among others, in spite of them.
Those who made fun of him ended up seeming grotesque to me…
